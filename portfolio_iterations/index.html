About

A bit about me

Photo

I'm a product designer with over a decade of experience crafting digital experiences across enterprise platforms, consumer applications, and everything in between. My work sits at the intersection of design thinking, systems design, and user advocacy.

Based in San Jose, California — when I'm not pushing pixels, you'll find me overlanding through the desert, chasing golden hour on dusty trails, and finding new perspectives in the landscapes that inspire my work.

I believe great design is invisible. It should feel effortless — even when the problems behind it are anything but.
